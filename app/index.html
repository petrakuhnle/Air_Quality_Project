Notes

Read with care

    Method

    • Hour view: mean for each hour of the selected period, days and stations.
    • Month view: monthly means for the selected stations and days. Click a month to select it; click it again for the full year.
    • Limits: mean of the selected period for every station, sorted by NO₂. For 2025 and all days this is the annual mean.
    • Days with fewer than 18 valid hours removed. Gaps are not filled. Times are the start of the hour, Berlin time.

    About the pollutants

    • Why NO₂ and PM2.5: they carry the largest health burden, and both have EU 2030 limits and WHO guideline levels to check each value against.
    • Why not ozone: ozone is not emitted directly. It forms from other pollutants in sunlight and is usually highest away from traffic, because exhaust near busy roads breaks it down again. Adding it would mix opposite patterns and complicate conclusions about where and when exposure is highest; it needs weather data to read correctly.
    • Also not included: the network measures more, for example PM10 and ozone. PM10 overlaps with PM2.5, its finest part. This project fetched NO₂ and PM2.5 only.
    • Possible next steps: ozone with weather data, traffic counts, more years for trends, and a satellite NO₂ comparison.
